I am a Ph.D. candidate in cybersecurity working at the intersection of security and artificial intelligence. My earlier work focused on access control, data security, and security policy; it later expanded to using NLP and large language models to understand complex security and privacy texts, as well as retrieval-augmented generation, fact-checking, and content safety. Across these topics, I study how data and knowledge can be used more safely, compliantly, and reliably in intelligent systems.
