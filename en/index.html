Hi, I'm Ziyan Zhou 😊, and my research interest is AI for Security.

My early research focused on access control, data security, and security policies. As my research progressed, I came to understand that cybersecurity involves technology, rules, and people. In data security and privacy protection in particular, a problem often requires both technical understanding and consideration of the requirements set out in data protection laws and regulations. This led me to explore how natural language processing and large language models can be applied to security and privacy problems to understand and evaluate complex security and privacy texts, gradually extending my research to retrieval-augmented generation, fact-checking, and content safety.

I am especially interested in how data and information can be used safely, compliantly, and reliably in intelligent systems. I hope to start from real-world problems, break down their complexity, and solve them using simple, effective methods.

My doctoral research and long-term project experience have taken me through the full process from technical investigation and algorithm design to system implementation and academic writing, and have helped me recognize the importance of clear thinking and expression. Looking ahead, I hope to work with people who enjoy exchanging ideas and inspiring one another, pursuing meaningful research or engineering grounded in real needs and growing together through continuous learning and sharing. I look forward to connecting with you!
