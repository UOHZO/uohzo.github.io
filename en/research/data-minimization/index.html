Paper notes

Research question

Data-minimization assessment in privacy policies requires both fine-grained extraction of data practices and compliance evidence that can be reviewed. This work asks how expert review costs can be reduced while maintaining evaluation accuracy and ethical compliance.

Method overview

LoRA fine-tuning and dense retrieval extract fine-grained data practices. Statistical associations propose candidate compliance mappings, which are validated by both a large language model and experts to distinguish inherent from contextual violations.
