Abstract

Rule construction is decomposed into four stages, supported by multi-task fine-tuning, curriculum learning, and Focal Loss.

Paper notes

Research question

Interaction rules for logical attack graphs are limited by predicate coverage, dependence on expert construction, and insufficient accuracy in automated construction. This work studies how natural-language vulnerability descriptions can become executable interaction rules.

Method overview

INTERNIST defines 19 general security predicates and decomposes rule construction into predicate extraction, argument linking, argument filling, and causal organization, supported by multi-task fine-tuning, curriculum learning, Focal Loss, and external knowledge.
