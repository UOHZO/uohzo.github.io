Abstract

A semantic hierarchy graph quantifies term vagueness, while contextual detection corrects pseudo-vagueness.

Paper notes

Research question

Existing privacy-policy vagueness assessment is limited by coarse analytical granularity, incomplete terminology coverage, and weak contextual awareness. VASH studies finer-grained quantification and contextual pseudo-vagueness detection.

Method and findings

A semantic hierarchy graph quantifies term vagueness. Context-aware pseudo-vagueness detection corrects term scores, and attention weighting produces sentence-level estimates. Experiments cover 75,145 privacy policies across 475 industries, with an F1 score of 0.82 for pseudo-vagueness detection.
