17:T4b5,When a research direction changes, what remains is often not a particular model or dataset, but a way of judging the problem.

## The shared question

From access control to privacy-policy compliance, the work repeatedly involves three actions: understanding rules, locating evidence, and deciding whether that evidence supports a conclusion. RAG and fact-checking bring the same actions into large-language-model systems.

## From rules to evidence

Privacy regulations are not merely keywords to match. Applicability conditions, atomic obligations, and concrete disclosures in a document need to be distinguished. Reliable retrieval likewise asks for more than a semantically similar passage: the evidence must actually answer the question.

## What remains open

One current question is whether a system can distinguish “the document did not disclose it” from “retrieval failed to find the disclosure.” This distinction matters both to trustworthy compliance assessment and to deciding when a model should continue retrieving or stop.
